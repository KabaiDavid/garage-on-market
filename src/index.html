01The Building

Once an auto service garage. Now one of NuLu’s favorite hangouts.

Garage Bar lives where horsepower meets happy hour. Our space celebrates Louisville’s roots with a fresh spin.

We’re serving up wood-fired pizza straight from the brick oven, craft cocktails featuring premium tequila, gin and vodka, and a rotating lineup of Kentucky bourbon that keeps things interesting.

Casual, social, and unapologetically local — this is where you grab a cold draft beer, catch the game, and stay late for that one more slice.

04Art at Garage

Slow collision.

It’s hard to miss the slow sounds of inevitable destruction coming from the sculpture outside Garage Bar. A machine advances two full-sized automobiles slowly into one another over time — simulating a head-on collision, each car moving almost imperceptibly toward the other.
